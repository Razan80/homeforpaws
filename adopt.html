Pets looking for a home

Every one of these pets is waiting at our partner shelter in Leumeah. Tap a pet to read their story, then enquire and we'll put you in touch with the shelter.

How adoption works

  1. Browse
    Find a pet whose personality fits your home.
  2. Enquire
    Send an enquiry from their profile. We pass it to the shelter.
  3. Meet
    Visit the shelter to meet them in person.
  4. Welcome home
    The shelter completes the adoption, and a new chapter begins.

Adoptions, fees and checks are handled by the shelter. We help their pets get seen.
