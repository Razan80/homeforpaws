How it works

  1. Tell us about your pet
    Fill in our quick, fun questionnaire with your dates.
  2. Free meet-and-greet
    Visit us so your pet can sniff around and meet us first.
  3. Drop off
    Bring food, bed and a favourite toy. We follow their routine.
  4. Daily updates
    Photos and news on your pet's own profile page.

What we need from you

Health

Up-to-date vaccinations (C5 for dogs, F3 for cats), flea, tick and worm treatment, and desexed for dogs over 6 months.

Peace of mind

Your vet's details and an emergency contact. If something goes wrong we go straight to the vet and call you.
